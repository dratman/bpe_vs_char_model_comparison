Avoid uneducated vernacular. Ralph corrected "where it's at" as uneducated.

**Why:** Ralph values precise, educated language. He notices and objects to
informal phrasing that would not pass muster in careful writing.

**How to apply:** Use standard English in all communication. Avoid colloquialisms,
slang, and grammatically loose constructions. Say "the current state" not
"where it's at." Say "the current progress" not "how things are going."
